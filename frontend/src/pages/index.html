Ancestral Provenance

From Loom to Heirloom

Witness the multi-generational devotion involved in crafting single drapes fit for royal courts.

01

Mulberry Sericulture

Harvesting Grade-A raw silk filament reels known for unparalleled tensile resilience and crystalline natural luster.

02

24K Pure Zari Gilding

Real silver wires flattened through diamond dies and electroplated in 24k melted gold bullion for eternal sheen.

03

Korvai Interlocking

Ancient technique requiring two weavers simultaneously linking heavy contrasting pallu borders to the body.

04

Guild Assaying

Every saree is tested for silver percentage, micro-hallmarked, and accompanied by physical silk mark certification.
